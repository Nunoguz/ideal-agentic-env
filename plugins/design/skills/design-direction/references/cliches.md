# Clichés of generic AI-made UI

Avoid these unless DESIGN.md deliberately chooses one, with a reason.

- Purple-to-blue gradients on the hero, buttons and text.
- Glassmorphism everywhere: blurred translucent cards on a gradient background.
- A centered hero with a big headline, a subline, two buttons, then three identical feature cards with emoji or generic line icons.
- Every element on `rounded-2xl` with a soft drop shadow; everything floats, nothing aligns to a grid.
- Inter or the system font at default tracking for everything, with no typographic hierarchy beyond size.
- Gray-on-gray low-contrast text "for elegance".
- Decorative blobs, sparkles and fake 3D illustrations unrelated to the product.
- Dashboards made of identical stat cards with a percentage badge and a sparkline.
- Dark mode as an inverted light theme: pure black backgrounds and saturated colors that vibrate.
- Placeholder copy ("Lorem ipsum", "Feature one", "Get started today!") and stock "happy team" imagery.
- Hover-only affordances and icon-only buttons with no labels.
- Animations on everything: fade-up on scroll for every block, bouncing buttons.
