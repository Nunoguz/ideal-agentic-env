# Legal bases (reference for proposals only; the DPO decides)

## KVKK (Law No. 6698) Art. 5: personal data

- **5(1) Explicit consent (açık rıza).** Freely given, specific and informed. It can be withdrawn, so do not rely on it when another basis applies.
- **5(2)** Processing without consent is allowed when one of these applies:
  - **(a)** It is explicitly provided for by law.
  - **(b)** It is necessary to protect the life or physical integrity of a person who cannot give consent.
  - **(c)** It is necessary for the establishment or performance of a contract with the data subject. This is typical for account and order data.
  - **(ç)** It is necessary for a legal obligation of the controller. Examples: invoices, tax and commercial records.
  - **(d)** The data subject made the data public.
  - **(e)** It is necessary to establish, exercise or protect a right.
  - **(f)** It is necessary for the controller's legitimate interests, without harming the data subject's fundamental rights. This requires a balancing test.

## KVKK Art. 6: special categories (özel nitelikli kişisel veri)

Race, ethnic origin, political opinion, philosophical belief, religion, sect or other belief, appearance and dress, membership of associations, foundations or trade unions, health, sexual life, criminal convictions and security measures, biometric and genetic data. These need explicit consent or one of the narrower conditions in Art. 6 (as amended in 2024). Always flag them to the DPO.

## GDPR Art. 6(1) (when EU residents' data is processed)

- (a) consent
- (b) contract
- (c) legal obligation
- (d) vital interests
- (e) public task
- (f) legitimate interests

Special categories fall under Art. 9.

## Other obligations to note in the activity

- Information notice (aydınlatma yükümlülüğü, KVKK Art. 10 / GDPR Art. 13–14).
- Transfers abroad (KVKK Art. 9: adequacy decision, appropriate safeguards such as standard contracts notified to the Board, binding corporate rules, or a listed exception).
- VERBİS registration, where the controller is obliged to register.
